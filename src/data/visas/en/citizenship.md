## New Zealand Citizenship

New Zealand citizenship is the culmination of your immigration journey — it grants you the full rights and responsibilities of a New Zealand national, including the ability to pass citizenship to your children and travel on a New Zealand passport.

## Eligibility Requirements

To apply for New Zealand citizenship by grant, you generally need to:

- Hold a residence visa (permanent or standard)
- Have been present in New Zealand for at least 1,350 days in the 5 years before applying
- Have been lawfully resident in NZ for the 12 months immediately before applying
- Be of good character
- Intend to continue living in New Zealand (or have close ties that make this reasonable)
- Meet any relevant English language requirements
