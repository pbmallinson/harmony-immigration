## Refugee & Humanitarian Visa

New Zealand has a proud tradition of providing refuge to those who need it most. We offer comprehensive, compassionate support to people seeking refugee or humanitarian protection in New Zealand.

## Our Commitment

This service is provided **free of charge**. We believe that everyone deserves access to expert immigration advice, regardless of their financial circumstances.

## Who We Help

- People who have fled persecution based on race, religion, nationality, political opinion, or social group membership
- Individuals in humanitarian circumstances who cannot return to their country of origin
- Those with family in New Zealand who are seeking refugee status
- People with approved refugee status seeking to bring family members to New Zealand

## What We Provide

- **Refugee status assessment** — An honest evaluation of your circumstances and the strength of your claim
- **Application preparation** — Full preparation of your asylum or humanitarian application
- **Interview preparation** — Coaching and support before any INZ or tribunal interviews
- **Appeal assistance** — If your initial claim is declined, we can advise on appeal options
- **Counselling referrals** — We can connect you with counselling and mental health support services
- **Community support** — Connections to local refugee support organisations and communities

## Confidentiality

All information you share with us is strictly confidential. We understand the sensitivity of these situations and treat every client with the utmost discretion and respect.
