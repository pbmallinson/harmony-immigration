Dr Edit Fellner-Andrejkovics immigrated to New Zealand nearly 20 years ago — an experience that gave her first-hand understanding of the challenges, hopes, and vulnerabilities that come with moving to a new country.

After building a career that spanned law, translation, and language teaching, Edit founded Harmony Immigration with a clear mission: to provide the kind of professional, genuinely caring immigration support she wished had been available to her when she first arrived.

A mother of two who established her business at age 34, Edit brings her entire professional expertise — PhD in Law, licensed immigration adviser status in both New Zealand and Australia, and NAATI translation certification — to every client relationship.

Her multilingual capability (English, Hungarian, Italian, and German) means she can provide support in the client's own language, removing one of the most significant barriers in the immigration process.

Above all, Edit is known for being honest, thorough, and genuinely invested in her clients' success.
