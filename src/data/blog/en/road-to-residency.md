For many of our clients, the dream of living in New Zealand begins years before the first visa application is lodged. This is the story of one family who took the leap — and made it.

## The Beginning

When Zsolt and Erzsébet first contacted Harmony Immigration, they had already spent two years researching New Zealand online, watching YouTube videos of Auckland sunsets, and telling their children about "the plan." They had a clear goal: a better quality of life, an excellent education for their kids, and the security of building something new together.

Zsolt was an experienced civil engineer with 15 years of experience. Erzsébet had worked as an accountant for a decade. On paper, they looked like ideal Skilled Migrant Category applicants.

## The Challenges

But immigration is never just "on paper." When we assessed their situation, several things needed careful attention:

- Zsolt's engineering qualifications needed to be assessed by Engineering New Zealand
- Erzsébet's accounting experience was solid, but she needed to understand exactly which NZ roles would be recognised
- Their two children's schooling requirements had to be factored into the timeline
- The family's savings needed to demonstrate sufficient settlement funds

## The Process

Over eight months, we worked together step by step. The qualifications assessment took three months alone. While they waited, we helped them research specific regions of New Zealand, connect with NZ civil engineering firms, and prepare their profiles for the job market.

When the Expression of Interest was selected, the speed of what followed surprised them both. Eighteen months after our first consultation, Zsolt, Erzsébet, and their two daughters received their residence visas.

## What They Said

"We are so grateful to Edit and the team at Harmony Immigration. The process felt overwhelming at the start, but having someone knowledgeable and genuinely caring on our side made all the difference. We are now in Tauranga — and it is everything we dreamed of."
