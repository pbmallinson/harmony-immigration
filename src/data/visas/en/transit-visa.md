## New Zealand Transit Visa

A Transit Visa allows certain travellers to pass through New Zealand on their way to another destination. It is a single-entry permit that covers your transit through a New Zealand airport.

## Do You Need One?

Many nationalities do not require a transit visa to pass through New Zealand. However, citizens of certain countries must obtain one before travel.

We can quickly confirm whether you need a transit visa and help you apply if required.
