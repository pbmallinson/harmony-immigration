## New Zealand Residence Visa

A Residence Visa is the pathway to making New Zealand your permanent home. It allows you to live, work, and study in New Zealand indefinitely, and is the step before New Zealand citizenship.

## Pathways to Residence

### Skilled Migrant Category (SMC)
A points-based system for people with skills in demand in New Zealand. Applicants submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) and are selected based on their score.

### Residence from Work
For holders of certain work visas who have been in New Zealand for a qualifying period and meet employment and character requirements.

### Family-Sponsored Residence
For partners, parents, and dependent children of New Zealand citizens or residents.

### Investor Residence
For individuals making a substantial investment in New Zealand.
