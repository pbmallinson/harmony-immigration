## New Zealand Student Visa

A Student Visa allows you to temporarily stay in New Zealand to attend an approved educational institution. It covers all levels of study, from primary school through to postgraduate university programmes.

## Who Needs One

All foreign nationals who wish to study in New Zealand for more than 3 months require a student visa. Citizens of certain countries studying for less than 3 months may be able to study on a visitor visa — we can advise you on your specific situation.
