## Language and Visa Preparation, Together

A strong English language result is a critical component of many New Zealand and Australian visa applications — particularly for residence and skilled migrant pathways. Our English & Visa Prep service combines targeted IELTS coaching with hands-on visa application preparation, so you're ready on both fronts.

## What's Included

- **IELTS preparation guidance** — Study strategies, practice materials, and coaching to help you achieve the scores you need.
- **English language requirement assessment** — Identifying exactly which tests and scores apply to your visa category.
- **Document preparation support** — Assistance gathering and presenting supporting evidence for your application.
- **Application review** — A full review of your completed application before it is submitted.

## Languages We Support

Dr Edit is a certified translator and practising interpreter in Hungarian and English, and also speaks Italian and German. We can support clients from a wide range of linguistic backgrounds.

## Ideal For

- Skilled migrants applying under the Skilled Migrant Category
- Students requiring English proficiency evidence
- Anyone preparing for a visa that includes language requirements
