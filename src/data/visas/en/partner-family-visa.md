## New Zealand Partner & Family Visa

New Zealand places great value on family unity. The Partner & Family Visa category provides pathways for the partners, parents, and dependent children of New Zealand citizens and permanent residents to join them in New Zealand.

## Partner Visas

### Partner of a New Zealand Citizen or Resident
Both married and de facto partners are eligible. You must demonstrate a genuine and stable relationship. Visa duration and conditions vary depending on whether you are applying from inside or outside New Zealand.

### Fiancé(e) Visa
If you intend to marry a New Zealand citizen or resident, you may be eligible to enter New Zealand on a fiancé(e) visa prior to marriage.

## Family Visas

### Parent Retirement Resident Visa
Allows parents of New Zealand citizens or residents to live permanently in New Zealand, provided they meet investment and English language requirements.

### Dependent Child Visa
For children under 24 who are dependent on a New Zealand citizen, resident, or certain visa holders.
