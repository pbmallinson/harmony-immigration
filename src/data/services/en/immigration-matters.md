## What We Do

Immigration law is complex and constantly evolving. At Harmony Immigration, we provide comprehensive legal guidance on all aspects of New Zealand and Australian immigration. Whether you're navigating a first-time visa application or dealing with a declined decision, Dr Edit Fellner-Andrejkovics brings her PhD in Law and years of frontline experience to every case.

## How We Help

- **Visa application preparation** — We prepare and review your entire application to maximise the chance of approval.
- **Immigration advice** — Clear, honest guidance on your options based on your specific circumstances.
- **Response to INZ correspondence** — We handle requests for further information and respond to issues raised by Immigration New Zealand.
- **Appeals and reconsiderations** — If your application has been declined, we can advise on appeal pathways.
- **Compliance reviews** — Ensuring your current visa conditions are being met and identifying any risks.

## Who This Is For

This service is suitable for individuals, families, and employers facing any immigration issue — whether straightforward or complex.
