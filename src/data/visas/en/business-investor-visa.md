## Business & Investor Visas

New Zealand actively welcomes entrepreneurs and investors who contribute to the economy. Our business immigration services cover the full spectrum of investor and entrepreneur visa pathways.

## Visa Types

### Active Investor Plus Visa
For high-net-worth individuals making a direct, active investment in New Zealand of at least NZD $5 million. Investments must be in acceptable categories such as companies, managed funds, or listed equities.

### Investor to Resident
A residence pathway for qualifying investors who have met their investment conditions.

### Entrepreneur Work Visa
For individuals setting up and operating a business in New Zealand. Requires a detailed business plan, proof of sufficient funds, and meeting character requirements.

### Entrepreneur Residence Visa
Available after 6 months on the Entrepreneur Work Visa, once your business is operational and meeting set performance criteria.
