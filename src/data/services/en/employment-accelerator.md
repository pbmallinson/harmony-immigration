## Fast-Track Your Career in New Zealand

The Employment Accelerator service is designed for skilled professionals who want to build their career in New Zealand. We combine immigration expertise with practical employment support to create the fastest, most secure pathway to your professional goals.

## What's Included

- **Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) guidance** — We walk you through the entire AEWV process, from finding an accredited employer to lodging your application.
- **Skills assessment support** — Advice on overseas skills recognition and how to present your qualifications to INZ and employers.
- **Job offer review** — Ensuring your job offer meets INZ requirements before you apply.
- **Employer liaison** — We communicate directly with your employer's HR team to ensure documentation is complete and correct.
- **Pathway to residence** — Planning the route from a work visa to long-term residence.

## Why It Matters

New Zealand's Accredited Employer Work Visa system has strict requirements — both for employers and for employees. A small error can result in delays or declines. We ensure every piece of your application is correctly prepared.
