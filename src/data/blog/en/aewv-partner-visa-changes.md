Immigration policy changes regularly, and keeping up with them is essential. Two significant changes have recently affected Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV) holders and their partners — here's what they mean for you.

## Changes to AEWV Eligibility

Immigration New Zealand has updated the minimum requirements for the Accredited Employer Work Visa. Key changes include:

### Wage Requirements
The median wage threshold has been updated. Your job offer must meet the current median wage to qualify for an AEWV in most occupations. Some sectors — particularly health and education — have different rules.

### Occupation Lists
The list of roles that allow AEWV holders to transition to residence has been updated. If your career goal is residence, it's worth confirming your occupation's status now, before investing significant time and money in a visa that may not lead where you want to go.

## Partner Visa Changes

Partners of AEWV holders have also been affected by recent changes. Previously, partners of most AEWV holders were eligible for an open work visa. Under the updated rules:

- Partners of AEWV holders earning **above the median wage** can continue to access an open work visa
- Partners of AEWV holders earning **at or below the median wage** may only be entitled to a visitor visa

This is a significant change for many families who had planned their finances around both partners working.

## What Should You Do?

If you or your partner holds an AEWV, or you are about to apply, I strongly recommend:

1. **Check your role against the current requirements** — don't rely on information that may be out of date
2. **Assess your partner's visa options** under the new rules before making major financial decisions
3. **Plan your pathway to residence** based on current rules, not assumptions
