## Certified Translation for Immigration

Immigration authorities require that all foreign-language documents be accompanied by a certified translation. As a NAATI-certified translator and member of the NZ Society of Translators and Interpreters (NZSTI), Dr Edit Fellner-Andrejkovics provides translations that are accepted by Immigration New Zealand, the New Zealand Qualifications Authority, and Australian immigration authorities.

## Language Pairs

- Hungarian ↔ English
- Italian → English
- German → English

## Documents We Translate

- Birth, marriage, and death certificates
- Academic transcripts and qualifications
- Police clearance certificates
- Employment contracts and references
- Court documents and legal correspondence
- Medical reports
- Identity documents (passports, national IDs)
- Business documents

## Certification

All translations are accompanied by a signed statement of accuracy and our NAATI certification details, making them fully accepted for official immigration purposes.
