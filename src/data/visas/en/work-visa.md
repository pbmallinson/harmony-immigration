## New Zealand Work Visas

A New Zealand work visa is a temporary permit issued by Immigration New Zealand (INZ) that allows foreign nationals to legally work in the country for a specified period. It is not a permanent right to live or work in New Zealand.

## Visa Types

### Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV)
The primary work visa for skilled migrants. Your employer must be accredited with INZ, and a job check must be completed confirming the role meets minimum requirements. This visa is tied to your specific employer and role.

### Investor Visa
For those making a significant investment in New Zealand. Requires meeting minimum investment thresholds and residence in New Zealand for a specified period.

### Entrepreneur Work Visa
Designed for people who wish to set up and run a business in New Zealand. Requires a business plan and meeting financial requirements.

### Working Holiday Visa
Available to citizens of select countries aged 18–30 (or 18–35 for some countries). Allows up to 12 months of travel and work in New Zealand.
