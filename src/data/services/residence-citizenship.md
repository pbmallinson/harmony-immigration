Residence and citizenship applications are usually the final step of a longer journey — and the one with the least room for error, since a knockback here can affect status you already hold.

## What's included

- Eligibility review against residence and citizenship criteria
- Character, health, and good-standing documentation support
- Full application preparation and lodging
- Citizenship test and interview preparation where required
- Guidance on maintaining status while your application is processed

## Who this is for

Long-term visa holders approaching residence eligibility, permanent residents ready to apply for citizenship, and families completing a multi-year immigration journey together.
