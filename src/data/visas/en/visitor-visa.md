## New Zealand Visitor Visa

A Visitor Visa allows you to visit New Zealand for tourism, to see family, or for short-term business purposes. It does not permit you to work or access public funds.

## Typical Reasons to Apply

- **Tourism** — Exploring New Zealand's landscapes and attractions
- **Family visits** — Visiting New Zealand citizen or resident family members
- **Business travel** — Attending meetings, conferences, or negotiations (not employment)
- **Medical treatment** — Receiving planned medical care in New Zealand

## Important Restrictions

A visitor visa does **not** allow you to:
- Take up any form of employment
- Enrol in a full-time educational programme longer than 3 months
- Access most New Zealand government-funded services
