## Arriving Is Just the Beginning

Getting your visa approved is a major achievement — but settling into a new country brings its own set of challenges. Our Settlement Support service provides ongoing guidance and practical help to ensure your transition to New Zealand life is as smooth and positive as possible.

## What We Offer

- **Pre-arrival planning** — Advice on housing, schooling, healthcare, and banking before you arrive.
- **IRD and government registration** — Help setting up your Inland Revenue number, bank accounts, and other essential registrations.
- **Community connections** — We can help connect you with local communities, particularly for Hungarian, Italian, and German-speaking newcomers.
- **School and education guidance** — Understanding the NZ school system and enrolling your children.
- **Ongoing immigration support** — Monitoring visa expiry dates, flagging upcoming renewals, and advising on pathways to long-term residence.
- **Ongoing check-ins** — We remain available as a point of contact for questions as they arise.

## Who Benefits

Settlement Support is especially valuable for:
- Families relocating with children
- Those who have never lived outside their home country
- People who don't speak English as a first language
- Anyone who simply wants the reassurance of an experienced guide during the first year in New Zealand
